The model: ConvNeXt-Base, fine-tuned for retina grading

The network starts from ConvNeXt-Base weights learned on millions of everyday photos (transfer learning), swaps in a new 5-class output layer, and trains the whole network on retina photos. Tap any stage to see what it does.

Why ConvNeXt

ConvNeXt is a convolutional network redesigned with ideas from vision transformers: large 7 x 7 kernels, LayerNorm and GELU. It sees wider context in each layer, which helps with retina photos where small lesions and overall vessel patterns both matter.

Why fine-tune everything

All 87.6 million weights are trained, but gently: AdamW at a small learning rate that decays along a cosine curve, label smoothing 0.1 and mixed precision. The network adapts to retina images without forgetting what it already knows.

What the output means

The last layer gives five raw scores. Softmax, applied when predicting, turns them into five probabilities that add up to 100%. The largest is the predicted stage and is shown as confidence.
